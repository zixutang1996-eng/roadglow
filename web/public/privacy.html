RoadGlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

RoadGlow is a personal, non-commercial map and navigation app. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking SDKs. The developer runs no server that receives your data.

Location

With your permission, RoadGlow uses your device location (precise and approximate) to show where you are, search nearby places and give turn-by-turn directions, including on Android Auto. During active navigation a foreground service with a visible notification keeps using location while the screen is off. Location is used on the device; it is not stored by or sent to the developer. RoadGlow does not request background location.

Requests to third-party map services

To draw maps, search and route, the app sends the needed data (map area, search text, and start/destination coordinates) directly from your device to public open services such as OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap Nominatim, Photon (Komoot), Overpass API, OSRM routing (routing.openstreetmap.de), Transitous, Open-Meteo, Wikidata/Wikimedia Commons, Panoramax, EOX and ipapi.co (approximate location when GPS is unavailable). These services receive your IP address and the request, under their own privacy policies. Updates and skin packs are downloaded from GitHub.

Data on your device

Favourites, history, settings, downloaded skins and offline maps are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Nothing is sold or shared by the developer.

Children

RoadGlow is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/zixutang1996-eng/roadglow.

隐私政策（中文）

RoadGlow 是个人非商业地图导航应用，无账号、无广告、无统计或追踪 SDK，开发者没有接收用户数据的服务器。经您允许，应用使用设备位置显示当前位置、搜索附近地点并导航（含 Android Auto）；导航时以带通知的前台服务持续定位。位置只在设备上使用，不会发给开发者；应用不申请后台定位。为显示地图、搜索和规划路线，应用会把必要信息（地图范围、搜索词、起终点坐标）直接发给上文列出的公开开放服务，这些服务会看到您的 IP 地址。收藏、历史、设置和离线地图只保存在您的设备上，卸载即删除。
